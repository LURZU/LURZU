# Hey, I'm Tom 👋
I used to spend my evenings on Minecraft. But instead of just playing, I wanted to **create** — add my own items, my own mechanics. I downloaded my first modding tools, opened code files without understanding a thing... and I loved it. That's when I first stepped into the world of development.

**Why Full Stack?** Because I like understanding how things work end-to-end. Not just the interface, not just the server — the whole picture. Turning an idea into a real product is what drives me.

**How I work?** 2 years of a Master's in Full Stack Development taught me to build projects from start to finish: design, development, deployment. I enjoy the rigor of back-end just as much as the creativity of front-end.

**What I'm looking for?** A team where I can learn, contribute, and grow. I know tech moves fast — that's exactly what I love about it. My goal: stay curious, adapt, and never stop improving.
